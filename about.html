About Us

Ragi Ruchulu is a local breakfast brand serving warm, familiar millet dishes in Jadcherla, Telangana. We focus on simple preparation, honest ingredients, and a welcoming morning experience.

Our breakfast stall is built around traditional taste, regular service, and timely preparation. We serve customers during the busy early morning hours and make it easy to order by phone or WhatsApp.

Whether you are stopping for a quick breakfast or ordering for your family, our goal is to make healthy, filling millet meals accessible and easy to enjoy.
